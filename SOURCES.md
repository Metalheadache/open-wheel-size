# Sources and attribution

No vehicle sources or claims are included in this tooling-only release.
`data/catalog.json` contains an empty `sources` table; `source-manifest.json`
is generated from that table. The excluded master retains all 2,390 sources
and their original citations. Their absence here does not mean they lack evidence.

Future included sources must retain stable source keys, public URLs, publisher,
title, source version, publication/retrieval precision and content hash where
available. Every claim must retain a section/page/table locator. Do not equate a
retrieval date, filename or Chinese translation with model-year/market scope.
Source documents are not bundled. Do not remove attribution to disguise origins.
An audited public-release basis and compatible licence/attribution scope are
required separately from the evidence citation.

Licence text sources are listed in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
